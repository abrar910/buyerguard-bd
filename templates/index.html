Key Capabilities

Why Pre-Purchase Verification Matters

In Bangladesh, land records are bifurcated between Sub-Registry deed archives and AC (Land) revenue ledgers. BuyerGuard BD organizes these simulated layers into an explainable verification snapshot.

Deed & Mutation Cross-Check

Organizes registered deed references alongside mutation (namjari) records to ensure current ownership claims match recorded revenue entries.

Double-Selling Simulator

Identifies simulated conflicts where multiple active sale claims or parallel deed conveyances exist for the same plot.

Completeness Scoring

Detects unrecorded fields such as unverified litigation or missing khas acquisition notices, preventing false assumptions of safety.

Printable Due Diligence

Generates a printable summary report paired with an interactive 7-point due diligence checklist for buyers to take to their legal counsel.

The Bangladesh Context

Navigating Multiple Land Documents

Purchasing immovable property in Bangladesh requires reviewing disparate records across multiple offices:

  • Original Title Deeds (Dalil):

    Registered at the Sub-Registry office establishing transfer of title.

  • Mutation Khatian (Namjari Porcha) & DCR:

    Recorded at the Assistant Commissioner (Land) office updating the revenue record.

  • Historical Survey Records (CS, SA, RS, City/BS):

    Verifying continuity of possession and chain of title across generations.

  • Physical Possession & Boundary Demarcation (Chouhaddi):

    Ground inspection to verify that the physical boundaries match the paper records.

Explainable Rule Engine Deterministic Python Logic
Unlike opaque black-box models, BuyerGuard BD evaluates every plot against 5 deterministic, verifiable rules:
Rule 1 (RULE_DUP_SALE): Multiple active sale claims with conflicting deeds.
Rule 2 (RULE_INCOMPLETE_RECORD): Missing essential deed, mutation, or khatian fields.
Rule 3 (RULE_REF_MISMATCH): Plot master deed conflicts with transaction registry.
Rule 4 (RULE_UNKNOWN_CASE): Litigation status unrecorded in demo dataset.
Rule 5 (RULE_UNKNOWN_KHAS): Khas / Government acquisition status unrecorded.
